# Global Electric Vehicle Market & Charging Infrastructure Analysis (2026)


# Project Overview

This project analyzes the global electric vehicle (EV) market and charging infrastructure for 2026 using Power BI. It focuses on understanding how factors like EV sales, battery performance, charging infrastructure, and government incentives influence adoption rates and environmental impact.

The project transforms raw data into interactive dashboards to uncover market trends, infrastructure impact, and sustainability outcomes.

# Dataset Description

The dataset includes comprehensive information about the EV ecosystem across multiple regions.

Key attributes include:

EV sales data

Vehicle types (cars, SUVs, etc.)

Battery capacity and vehicle range

Charging time and infrastructure availability

Government incentives

EV adoption rate

CO₂ emission reduction

# Objectives

Analyze EV sales performance by country, region, brand, and vehicle type

Evaluate the impact of charging infrastructure on EV adoption

Compare battery capacity, vehicle range, and charging time

Study the influence of government incentives on market growth

Measure environmental benefits through CO₂ emission reduction

# Dashboard Features

EV sales analysis by region and vehicle type

Charging infrastructure vs adoption rate visualization

Battery capacity and vehicle range comparison

Charging time impact analysis

CO₂ emission reduction insights

Interactive filters for region, vehicle type, and brand

# Dataset Information

Format: CSV

Type: Structured dataset

Year: 2026

Domain: Electric Vehicles & Sustainability

Usage: Data used for building Power BI dashboards and insights

# Tools & Skills Used

Power BI – Dashboard development & data visualization

Data Visualization – Interactive reports and storytelling

# Key Insights

Higher charging station availability leads to increased EV adoption

Faster charging and longer battery range improve consumer acceptance

Cars and SUVs dominate EV sales globally

Government incentives significantly boost EV market growth

EV adoption contributes to reducing CO₂ emissions

# Project Outcomes

Highlighted the importance of infrastructure and policy support

Strengthened data visualization and analytical skills

# Conclusion

This project demonstrates that the growth of the EV market is driven by charging infrastructure, supportive government policies, and advancements in battery technology.

Regions investing in these areas show higher adoption rates and sales. The analysis highlights both economic and environmental benefits, making EV adoption a key factor in achieving sustainability goals.
